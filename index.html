Al Murattil Quran Institute

Learning the Quran with clarity, care and proper guidance.

Al Murattil Quran Institute provides structured Quran learning for students who wish to strengthen their recitation, Tajweed, memorisation and understanding of the Quran.

About the Institute

Al Murattil Quran Institute is dedicated to helping students develop strong Quran reading and recitation skills through organised lessons, personal guidance and a supportive learning environment.

Our Learning Approach

Our programs guide students step by step, beginning with foundational Quran reading and progressing into Tajweed, fluent recitation and memorisation.

Learning for Different Levels

Courses are designed for students at different stages of Quran learning. Students can review available programs, schedules and course information before registration.
