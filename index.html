Learning the Qur'an with clarity, care and proper guidance.

Al Murattil Quran Institute provides structured Qur'an learning for students who want to strengthen their recitation, Tajweed, memorisation and confidence in reading the Qur'an.

Purposeful Qur'an learning

About the Institute

Al Murattil Quran Institute is dedicated to helping students develop strong Qur'an reading and recitation skills through organised lessons and guided learning.

Our Learning Approach

Students are guided step by step, from foundational reading skills to Tajweed, fluent recitation and memorisation according to their learning level.

Learning for Different Levels

The institute offers programmes for learners at different stages, allowing students to choose a course suited to their current ability and goals.
